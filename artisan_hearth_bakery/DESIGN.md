## Brand & Style

This design system is tailored for an artisanal hearth bakery nestled in Songpa (Bangi & Seokchon), dedicated strictly to rustic meal breads—crusty baguettes, stone-ground whole wheat loaves, and slow-fermented ciabatta.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Intent
- **Integrity & Principle:** Rooted in minimal ingredients (flour, water, salt, levain) and uncompromising long fermentation. The UI must project timeless discipline rather than transient cafe trends.
- **Warm Groundedness:** Welcoming, tactful, and domestic. Evokes early morning baker's benches, rustic linen table cloths, and freshly sliced hearth loaves.
- **Craftsmanship & Sincerity:** Unhurried, deliberate, and architectural. The interface communicates dependability and daily nourishment for local tables.

### Design Movement
- **Editorial Tactile Minimalism:** Combines clean, ample negative space with tangible, organic warmth. Interfaces eschew synthetic gloss, plastic gradients, and hyper-saturated accents, choosing instead natural parchment textures, precise rule lines, and structured editorial hierarchy reminiscent of a curated culinary publication or heritage grain ledger.

## Colors

The palette takes direct cues from the hearth baking process: scorched crusts, stone-milled wheat berries, raw linen couches, and dried culinary herbs.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#3E2718` — Crust Dark Brown):** Dense, deep, and caramelized. Used for essential typographic hierarchy, primary actions, and primary grounding dividers. Replaces synthetic black with organic depth.
- **Secondary (`#C68B59` — Golden Oven Crust):** Warm toasted amber representing baked exterior tones. Reserved for baking status tags, accents, active bread batch badges, and focus rings.
- **Tertiary (`#7A8267` — Wild Sage & Olive):** Muted, dried herb green. Signifies naturally fermented sourdough levain, origin traceability tags, and organic grain sourcing certifications.
- **Neutral (`#F4EDE2` — Warm Wheat Beige):** The foundational ambient background tone. Provides tactile warmth and softness beneath natural linen card surfaces.
- **Surface Canvas (`#FCFBF9` — Natural Linen White):** Pure unbleached flour/linen tone utilized for surface elevations, input plates, and elevated recipe cards.

### Foreground & Contrast Ratios
- High contrast pairing of `#3E2718` on `#FCFBF9` (exceeding 12:1) ensures effortless legibility for long-form bread descriptions, ingredient transparency lists, and mobile ordering flows.

## Typography

The typographic pairing contrasts the artisanal weight of traditional European bakeries with the clarity required for digital menus and pickup schedules.

- **Headlines (Source Serif 4):** A sturdy, authoritative serif with grounded bracketed serifs and modest proportions. It evokes vintage European flour sacks and classic recipe books while preserving sharp digital rendering.
- **Body & Functional UI (Work Sans):** A balanced, unpretentious grotesque sans serif. Highly legible at compact sizes, making ingredient labels, hydration percentages, fermentation durations, and pickup time slots immediately scannable.
- **Letter Spacing & Case:** Label tokens apply deliberate uppercase styling paired with expanded tracking (`0.04em` to `0.08em`) to mirror archival batch numbering and bakery stamping.

## Layout & Spacing

### Grid Philosophy
The system implements a structured 12-column fluid grid on desktop (max width `1280px`), transitioning to an 8-column layout on tablets and a 4-column layout on mobile devices.

### Breakpoints & Responsive Behavior
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout, `1rem` gutters, and `1.25rem` screen margins. Bread schedule tiers reflow into a linear vertical stream. Detail tables shift into stacked cards.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8-column layout, `1.5rem` gutters, and `2rem` screen margins. Allows two-column bakery inventory grids and side-by-side ingredient disclosure.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout centered with `3rem` margins. Editorial spreads feature asymmetrical layouts, juxtaposing wide-aspect process imagery against structured bread specifications.

### Rhythmic Cadence
Components observe strict rhythmic multiples of `8px` (`0.5rem`). Dense informational regions (e.g., fermentation time stamps, grain composition breakdowns) compress using `space-xs` and `space-sm`, while editorial bread showcase sections breathe through `space-lg` and `space-xl`.

## Elevation & Depth

### Tactile Layering Over Shadows
This design system rejects deep drop shadows and synthetic floating blurs, choosing instead physical paper stacking, tonal stratification, and crisp structural borders.

- **Base Canvas:** `#F4EDE2` (Warm Wheat Beige) establishes the tactile foundation.
- **Level 1 (Card & Content Surface):** `#FCFBF9` (Natural Linen White) rests flatly upon the canvas, delineated by a subtle 1px border colored `#E5DACB` (`#3E2718` at 12% alpha).
- **Level 2 (Dropdowns, Sheets, & Dialogs):** `#FCFBF9` bordered with `#D7C7B2`, reinforced solely by an ambient, diffused grounding shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(62, 39, 24, 0.08)`.
- **Structural Dividing Rules:** Hairline separators (`1px solid rgba(62, 39, 24, 0.14)`) organize sections, mirroring traditional editorial broadsheets and ledger books.

## Shapes

### Form Geometry: Soft (Level 1)
To reflect the deliberate precision of artisan baking tools (couches, proofing bannetons, scoring lames, and stone decks), corners remain architectural, crisp, and disciplined:

- **Base Radius (`0.25rem` / 4px):** Buttons, inputs, chips, and small alert badges. Preserves structure without feeling abrasive.
- **Container Radius (`rounded-lg` - `0.5rem` / 8px):** Loaf showcase cards, recipe sheets, and modal frames.
- **Feature Radius (`rounded-xl` - `0.75rem` / 12px):** Reserved solely for primary hero viewports and interactive product tasting boards.
- **Circular Elements:** Strictly reserved for status dots (e.g., "Daily Bake Ready", "Proofing in Progress") and batch quantity counters.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#3E2718` background, `#FCFBF9` text, `4px` corner radius. Hover shifts subtly to `#533522` with a 150ms ease transition. Active states scale down to `98%`.
- **Secondary / Outlined:** Transparent background, `1.5px` border in `#3E2718`, `#3E2718` text. Hover fills to `rgba(62, 39, 24, 0.05)`.
- **Ghost / Tertiary:** No border, `#3E2718` text with an underline offset of `4px`. Hover state tints text to `#C68B59`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status Tags (e.g., "Fresh Out of Oven", "Sourdough Starter 2018"):** Compact padding (`4px 10px`), uppercase `label-sm` tracking, background of `rgba(198, 139, 89, 0.15)` with `#3E2718` text.
- **Grain Origin & Organic Badges:** Background of `rgba(122, 130, 103, 0.15)`, text color `#474E39`, adorned with a minimalist botanical icon.

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Input Fields:** Crisp `#FCFBF9` surface with a `1px` border of `rgba(62, 39, 24, 0.2)`. On focus, transitions to a `1.5px` border of `#3E2718` with a soft halo of `rgba(198, 139, 89, 0.2)`. Labels adopt `label-md` seated neatly above the field.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Sharp `2px` corners for checkboxes; circular for radios. In checked states, surfaces fill with `#3E2718` featuring a clean `#FCFBF9` checkmark.

### Cards (Product & Loaf Profiles)
- **Structure:** Layered `#FCFBF9` container with a fine border (`#E5DACB`).
- **Layout:** High-ratio product photography displaying crust texture and crumb aeration, anchored by a `Source Serif 4` title, hydration level percentage, flour composition tags, and daily baking timetable.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Bake Schedule Timeline:** Linear step-component depicting daily rack releases (e.g., 08:30 Country Sourdough, 11:00 Traditional Baguettes, 13:30 Olive Ciabatta). Employs warm golden points (`#C68B59`) that shift to `#7A8267` once baked and ready for pickup.
- **Grain & Hydration Spec Table:** Ledger-style tabular module showing percentage of stone-ground whole wheat, levain age, and hydration percentage, styled with hairline horizontal borders and `label-md` metadata headers.
